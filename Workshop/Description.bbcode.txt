[h1]Pre-release Note[/h1]
[b]So far it has been tested only on my own PC[/b] (Windows 11, RTX 5070 Ti), so expect some bugs and rough edges, and please report anything unusual (see [b]Reporting a bug[/b] below).
[b]!!Works only on Windows!![/b] On Mac and Linux, the shaders won't work, so it will fall back to another rendering method which doesn't look good.

[h1]How to use[/h1]
[olist]
[*][b]Subscribe[/b] to this mod and to Harmony, then make sure both are enabled in [i]Content Manager → Mods[/i]. If you use Skyve, make sure the mod is enabled in it.
[*][b]Load a city[/b] (new or saved) and the clouds appear. The mod does not run in the editors or in scenarios.
[*][b]Press F4[/b] (or click the mod's button in UUI) to open the in-game panel.
[/olist]

To see more tabs in the in-game UI like the screenshots above, press [i]Esc[/i] to access the Pause Menu, then [i]Options[/i]. Under "Mods Settings", find Volumetric Weather, then go to [i]General[/i] and toggle "Show advanced options in the in-game panel".

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Volumetric 3D clouds[/b]
[*][b]Real cloud shadows[/b]
[*][b]Follows the game's weather[/b], and overridable
[*][b]Rain that comes from the clouds[/b]
[*][b]Lightning[/b] that lights up the clouds from inside
[*][b]Volumetric fog[/b] (optional, off by default): fog that rolls over the city like low cloud
[*][b]Better nights[/b]: clouds have a soft glow on their undersides, just like in real life
[*][b]Smaller light halos[/b] (optional, off by default): shrinks the oversized glow around street lamps, building lights and vehicles
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Performance[/h1]
The clouds are drawn in 3D every frame, so they cost more than the game's flat sky; how much depends on your graphics card.
[list]
[*][b]Volumetric fog is very heavy.[/b] It is by far the most expensive part of the mod, which is why it is off by default.
[*]If the clouds alone are too demanding, lower the [i]Quality preset[/i] (Options → Volumetric Weather → Rendering) to Medium or Low.
[*]The Medium and Low presets are estimates for now, not yet measured on slower hardware. Reports of how they run are welcome.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
I develop and test this mod in my own game. No setup covers every combination, so please report any conflict you find.
[b]Removing the mod[/b] should not corrupt your saves: the game simply keeps the mod's small block of sky data, unread. That said, I cannot be held responsible for anything that goes wrong, lost or damaged saves included, so please keep backups.

[h1]Reporting a bug[/h1]
Please include the following:
[olist]
[*][b]The game's log[/b], [i]output_log.txt[/i]. In Steam, right-click Cities: Skylines → [i]Manage → Browse local files[/i], then open the [i]Cities_Data[/i] folder.
[*][b]The mod's own log[/b], [i]VolumetricClouds.log[/i]. Tick [i]Detailed logging[/i] (Options → Volumetric Weather → General) first, then make the problem happen again. To find it, paste this into File Explorer's address bar:
[code]%LOCALAPPDATA%\Colossal Order\Cities_Skylines[/code]
[*][b]What happened[/b]: what you did and expected, screenshots, your graphics card and your other mods.
[/olist]
Both logs start fresh at every launch, so copy them before restarting, upload them (Google Drive, Pastebin...) and post the link in the comments.
