[hr][/hr]
[h1]Overview[/h1]
Volumetric Weather adds real 3D clouds to Cities: Skylines. They drift over your city with the wind, cast real shadows on the ground and follow the game's weather: a clear day brings a few scattered clouds, a rainy day brings heavy grey ones, with rain falling from them and lightning flashing inside.

No setup is needed: subscribe, load a city, done.

[b]Everything can be overridden.[/b] Every feature can be switched off or adjusted, cloud, fog and lightning can each be set by hand whatever the weather, and where the mod replaces part of the game (its rain, its lightning bolt), a switch brings the original back.

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Volumetric clouds[/b] you can fly through, not a flat picture of a sky.
[*][b]Real cloud shadows[/b] moving across your buildings and terrain.
[*][b]Follows the game's weather[/b]: random weather, disasters and Play It!'s weather sliders.
[*][b]Rain that comes from the clouds[/b]: it falls only under raining clouds, and the rain sound and wet roads follow it.
[*][b]Lightning[/b] that lights up the clouds from inside, followed a moment later by thunder. The extra storm lightning is purely visual and never starts fires.
[*][b]Volumetric fog[/b] (optional, off by default): fog that rolls over the city like low cloud, with sunbeams through the gaps and a glow around the city's lights at night.
[*][b]Better nights[/b]: clouds hide the stars behind them and have a soft glow on their undersides.
[*][b]Smaller light halos[/b] (optional, off by default): shrinks the oversized glow around street lamps, building lights and vehicles (Intersection Marking Tool lamps included), with no need for Persistent Fog Adjuster.
[*][b]Your city keeps its sky[/b]: the cloud pattern and the wind are saved with your city, so it looks the same when you return.
[/list]

[h1]How to use[/h1]
Only the first two steps are required.
[olist]
[*][b]Subscribe[/b] to this mod and to Harmony, then make sure both are enabled in [i]Content Manager → Mods[/i].
[*][b]Load a city[/b] (new or saved) and the clouds appear. The mod does not run in the editors or in scenarios.
[*][b]Press F4[/b] (or click the mod's button) to open the in-game panel.
[*][b]All other settings[/b] (rain, lightning, shadows, quality, halos, the F4 key) are on the mod's page in [i]Options → Mod Settings[/i].
[*][b]To start over[/b], use [i]Options → Volumetric Weather → General → Reset all settings to defaults[/i].
[/olist]
The tabs of the in-game panel:
[list]
[*][b]Now[/b]: the current weather and the overrides for cloud, fog and lightning. [i]Follow the game's weather[/i] clears them all; [i]Test lightning[/i] shows a strike.
[*][b]Clouds[/b]: the height, thickness and break-up of the clouds.
[*][b]Fog[/b]: the volumetric fog switch and its look (read [b]Performance[/b] first).
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Performance[/h1]
The clouds are drawn in 3D every frame, so they cost more than the game's flat sky; how much depends on your graphics card.
[list]
[*][b]Volumetric fog is very heavy.[/b] It is by far the most expensive part of the mod, which is why it is off by default.
[*]If the clouds alone are too demanding, lower the [i]Quality preset[/i] (Options → Volumetric Weather → Rendering) to Medium or Low.
[*]The Medium and Low presets are estimates for now, not yet measured on slower hardware. Reports of how they run are welcome.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
I develop and test this mod in my own game, which runs thousands of mods and assets, and no conflicts have been detected. Mods that change the sky, the lighting or the weather were checked specifically: Render It!, Theme Mixer, Play It! and cloud replacers all work alongside it.
No setup covers every combination, so please report any conflict you find.
For reference: the mod takes the place of the game's rain and rain-cloud layer (its lightning and thunder are kept) and, with volumetric fog on, of its foggy-weather effect. It reads the game's weather but never changes it. On winter maps the game's snow is left as it is.
[b]Removing the mod[/b] should not corrupt your saves: the game simply keeps the mod's small block of sky data, unread. That said, I cannot be held responsible for anything that goes wrong, lost or damaged saves included, so please keep backups.

[h1]Reporting a bug[/h1]
Please include the following:
[olist]
[*][b]The game's log[/b], [i]output_log.txt[/i]. In Steam, right-click Cities: Skylines → [i]Manage → Browse local files[/i], then open the [i]Cities_Data[/i] folder.
[*][b]The mod's own log[/b], [i]VolumetricClouds.log[/i]. Tick [i]Detailed logging[/i] (Options → Volumetric Weather → General) first, then make the problem happen again. To find it, paste this into File Explorer's address bar:
[code]%LOCALAPPDATA%\Colossal Order\Cities_Skylines[/code]
[*][b]What happened[/b]: what you did and expected, screenshots, your graphics card and your other mods.
[/olist]
Both logs start fresh at every launch, so copy them before restarting, upload them (Google Drive, Pastebin...) and post the link in the comments.
