[h1]How to use[/h1]
[olist]
[*][b]Subscribe[/b] to this mod and to Harmony, then make sure both are enabled in [i]Content Manager → Mods[/i]. If you use Skyve, make sure the mod is enabled in it.
[*][b]Load a city[/b] (new or saved) and the clouds appear. The mod does not run in the editors or in scenarios.
[*][b]Press F4[/b] (or click the mod's button in UUI) to open the in-game panel.
[/olist]

To see more tabs in the in-game UI like the screenshots above, press [i]Esc[/i] to access the Pause Menu, then [i]Options[/i]. Under "Mods Settings", find Volumetric Weather, then toggle "Show advanced options in the in-game panel".

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Features[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Volumetric 3D clouds with real shadows[/b] on the ground
[*][b]Follows the game's weather[/b], or you can set it yourself
[*][b]Rain and lightning that come from the clouds[/b]: rain falls only under the clouds, and lightning lights them up from inside
[*][b]NEW: Cloud colours[/b]: pick one colour for the sunlit side of the clouds and one for the shaded side, from warm sunsets to an orange city glow at night. White keeps the normal look
[*][b]Better nights[/b]: clouds have a soft glow on their undersides, just like in real life
[*][b]Optional, off by default[/b]: [b]volumetric fog[/b] that rolls over the city like low cloud, and [b]smaller light halos[/b] around street lamps, building lights and vehicles
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Performance[/h1]
Based on player feedback, the clouds run great even on low-end PCs. And you can always turn the settings down:
[list]
[*][b]Volumetric fog is very heavy.[/b] It is by far the most expensive part of the mod, which is why it is off by default.
[*]If the clouds are too demanding, lower the [i]Quality preset[/i] to Medium or Low. It is on the in-game panel's [i]Rendering[/i] tab, which appears once [i]Show advanced options in the in-game panel[/i] is ticked (Options → Volumetric Weather).
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
Tested together with [b]AD Cloud Replacer[/b], [b]Render It![/b], [b]Theme Mixer[/b], [b]Play It![/b] and [b]Intersection Marking Tool[/b], with no conflicts. No setup covers every combination, so please report any conflict you find.
[b]Removing the mod[/b] should not corrupt your saves: the game simply keeps the mod's small block of sky data, unread. That said, I cannot be held responsible for anything that goes wrong, lost or damaged saves included, so please keep backups.

[h1]Reporting a bug[/h1]
Please include the following:
[olist]
[*][b]The game's log[/b], [i]output_log.txt[/i]. In Steam, right-click Cities: Skylines → [i]Manage → Browse local files[/i], then open the [i]Cities_Data[/i] folder.
[*][b]The mod's own log[/b], [i]VolumetricClouds.log[/i]. Tick [i]Detailed logging[/i] (Options → Volumetric Weather) first, then make the problem happen again. To find it, paste this into File Explorer's address bar:
[code]%LOCALAPPDATA%\Colossal Order\Cities_Skylines[/code]
[*][b]What happened[/b]: what you did and expected, screenshots, your graphics card and your other mods.
[/olist]
Both logs start fresh at every launch, so copy them before restarting, upload them (Google Drive, Pastebin...) and post the link in the comments.
