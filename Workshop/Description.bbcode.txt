[h1]Version 1.5[/h1]
[h3]New cloud styles: Mixed & Soft[/h3]
These new styles are more demanding, recommended only for users with powerful hardware.
[h3]Day, sunset and night, clear sky and overcast[/h3]
A lot of options now get different sliders for time of day, and for overcast conditions.
[h3]Changing fog, and waterspouts[/h3]
Morning fog, mist after rain, fog in valleys and fog banks over water, with the chance of a small waterspout under rain over the sea.
[h3]New panel styles[/h3]
3 new looks for the in-game panel, with the new Frosted Glass theme as the default.

See everything in the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/3805863507]change notes[/url].
If something stopped working after the update, please let me know.

[h1]How to use[/h1]
[olist]
[*][b]Subscribe[/b] to [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2040656402]Harmony[/url] and then this mod, make sure both are enabled. If you use Skyve, make sure the mod is enabled in it.
[*][b]Load a city[/b] (new or saved) and the volumetric weather should appear. The mod does not run in the editors or in scenarios.
[*][b]Press F4[/b] (or click the mod's button) to open the in-game panel.
[/olist]

To see more tabs in the in-game UI like the screenshots above, go to the mod's options in the Pause Menu, then toggle "Show advanced options in the in-game panel".

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Features[/h1]
[h3]Clouds[/h3]
[list]
[*]Volumetric 3D clouds with real shadows, in four styles: Classic, Cumulus, Soft and Mixed
[*]Distant clouds, light inside the clouds and cloud fragments
[/list]
[h3]Weather[/h3]
[list]
[*]Follows the game's weather, or set it yourself
[*]Rain and lightning that come from the clouds, rainbows, and waterspouts
[/list]
[h3]Fog and light (optional)[/h3]
[list]
[*]Volumetric fog that rolls over the city, changes with the weather and the time of day, fills valleys and drifts in from the water
[*]Every setting can have its own value at sunset, at night and under an overcast sky
[*]Smaller light halos around lamps, buildings and vehicles (off by default)
[/list]

[h1]Reporting a bug[/h1]
Please include the following:
[olist]
[*][b]The game's log[/b], [i]output_log.txt[/i]. In Steam, right-click Cities: Skylines → [i]Manage → Browse local files[/i], then open the [i]Cities_Data[/i] folder. On a Mac it is [i]~/Library/Logs/Unity/Player.log[/i], on Linux [i]~/.config/unity3d/Colossal Order/Cities_Skylines/Player.log[/i].
[*][b]The mod's own log[/b], [i]VolumetricClouds.log[/i]. Tick [i]Detailed logging[/i] (Options → Volumetric Weather) first, then make the problem happen again. To find it, paste this into File Explorer's address bar:
[code]%LOCALAPPDATA%\Colossal Order\Cities_Skylines[/code]
On a Mac it is in [i]~/Library/Application Support/Colossal Order/Cities_Skylines[/i], on Linux in [i]~/.local/share/Colossal Order/Cities_Skylines[/i].
[*][b]What happened[/b]: what you did and expected, screenshots, your graphics card and your other mods.
[/olist]
Both logs start fresh at every launch, so copy them before restarting, upload them (Google Drive, Pastebin...) and post the link in the comments.
