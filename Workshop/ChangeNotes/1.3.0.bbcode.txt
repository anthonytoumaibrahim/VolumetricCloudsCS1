[h1]Version 1.3.0[/h1]
[b]New[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Profiles[/b]: save whole skies under a name and switch between them from the list at the top of the in-game panel.
[*][b]Rainbows[/b]: an arch standing in a sunlit shower near where you look. Strength on the Weather tab, a chance per rain on the Now tab.
[*][b]Night colours[/b]: the clouds can have colours of their own at night. Off by default, on the Clouds tab.
[*][b]Night brightness[/b]: the clouds can have a brightness of their own at night. Off by default, on the Light tab.
[*][b]Snow[/b]: on winter maps the snow now falls only under the clouds, with the game's own snowflakes, and road snow follows it.
[*][b]Cloud position[/b]: move a city's clouds up to 10 km each way, saved with the city (Weather tab, advanced options on).
[*][b]Classic layer[/b]: how much of the Classic layer fills in among the Cumulus heaps (Clouds tab).
[/list]
[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*]The clouds block the sun: the sun and its glow no longer shine through the clouds in front of them.
[*]Lightning bolts are no longer cut off by the clouds behind them.
[*]Power lines, street lamp glow and smoke no longer fade into the clouds behind them.
[*]The game's hazy band above the horizon no longer hides the distant clouds, and at night the clouds no longer fade out 10 km away.
[*]Without the Unified UI mod, the mod no longer adds a Unified UI panel of its own.
[/list]
[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*]The volumetric fog is much less grainy. At the High preset it costs more.
[*]The [i]Light[/i] tab is always in the in-game panel.
[*]Your settings and saved cities are unchanged.
[/list]
[b]Removed[/b]
[list]
[*]The [i]Rendering[/i] tab: its settings are now at the top of the mod's options page (Esc, Options, Volumetric Weather).
[/list]
